Built to spec

The numbers behind a good lot

4″Standard line widthThe width used for parking stall lines on most commercial lots.
9×18Typical stall, in feetA common standard size. We confirm against your city's code before we paint.
96″Van-accessible aisleA van space needs an 8-foot access aisle beside it (or an 11-foot space with a 5-foot aisle).
2–3Years between seal coatsThe usual interval for a lot with regular traffic in the Southern heat.
24HRTypical time before trafficSeal coat usually cures overnight in warm, dry weather, so most lots reopen the next day.
